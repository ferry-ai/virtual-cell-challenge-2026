I'll audit GSE335887 and the McQuade paper from public sources only, starting with the GEO record and the article.GSE335887 can support a held-out-gene test of graded versus threshold versus responder-fraction responses, but the paper’s binary-versus-graded result uses a score built from the same transcriptome as the response.

**1. Files.** No sub-series (**verified**, GEO relations empty: https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/esummary.fcgi?db=gds&id=200335887). One series, public date 2026-06-18, supplementary types CSV, H5, H5AD, H5MU; eight samples, each summary “WTC11” (**verified**, same esummary). FTP listing https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE335nnn/GSE335887/suppl/: `iMG_processed_cite_crop.h5mu` 520M, `iTF_processed_cite_crop.h5mu` 778M, `iTF_processed_merged_crop.h5ad` 2.7G; Cell Ranger matrices `img` filtered 105M / raw 163M and `itf` filtered 139M / raw 195M; two feature-reference csv.gz at 4.3K. The SRA study title still says the GEO accession is private until 2027-06-15 (**verified** contradiction, unresolved). Spots: CITE iTF GEX 609,218,300, ADT 119,712,109, sgRNA 80,538,976; CITE iMG GEX 449,425,598, ADT 102,347,316, sgRNA 79,399,510; extra CROP iTF GEX 1,568,154,562 and sgRNA 1,549,194,716. Library: 31 genes × 2 sgRNAs plus 5 non-targeting = 65; iTF-MG CROP-seq is two biological replicates sequenced separately then merged; load is 40,000 cells/lane, one lane per sample, 10x v4 (**verified**, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13505846/). CITE-seq ADT is public; the paper correlates 166 surface proteins. Cells per perturbation are not in the prose (it points to Table S2, not opened). Guide assignment is described (geomux) and filenames imply it is stored; **unverified** inside the h5/h5mu. sgRNA calling used a 10x 3′ v3.1 barcode whitelist while loading text says v4.

**2. Binary versus graded.** Custom Mixscale gives each cell a Z-score from a differential-expression vector on the full transcriptome (Benjamini–Hochberg instead of Bonferroni). For ZNF532 knockdown in iMG, the disease-associated signature is bimodal: low score matches non-targeting cells, a moderate score is already near maximal. PRDM1 is not on/off: genes move with the score, and downregulated genes move at lower scores than upregulated ones. Two guides (g1, g2) are shown as median UCell shifts and densities (**verified**, same PMC). **Inferred circularity:** the score uses the same transcriptome as the state signatures. The authors say this is needed because some transcription-factor targets are too low to detect in single-cell RNA-seq. They do not report a score fit on genes disjoint from the outcome.

**3. Knockdown.** Arrayed follow-up lines use qPCR, ΔΔCt versus GAPDH, cited as Figure S2 (**verified**, methods). Prose only: PRDM1 g2 stronger than g1; DNMT1 reduced “modestly”; STAT1 and IRF9 “more modest.” No numeric on-target percent for each of the 62 targeting guides in the text read. CITE-seq is a surface panel, not the transcription-factor protein. In the pool, intensity is Mixscale, not target UMIs.

**4. Terms.** GEO: NCBI places no restrictions, but submitters may claim intellectual property (**verified**, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html). No series-specific licence found. The paper is an NIHMS author manuscript in *Neuron*, not stated here as CC-BY. Cell lines need an MTA. Code: Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20517618 and github.com/reetm09; code licence **unverified**.
