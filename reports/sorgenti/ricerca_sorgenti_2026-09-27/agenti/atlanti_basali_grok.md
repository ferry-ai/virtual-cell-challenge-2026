## 4. Basal profiles of D, E, and F before 22 October

No page read here names a tissue or a line for D, E, or F, and none publishes their basal profiles before 22 October 2026.

**Verified** on https://arcinstitute.org/news/virtual-cell-challenge-2026 (opened): six lines from different tissues of origin; CRISPRi; 10x Flex; Ultima UG100; three lines for validation and three held for the final test; for each line, participants receive non-targeting profiles as the basal state; timeline item “Thursday, October 22: Final test set released.”

**Verified** on https://vcc-cli-wiki.virtualcellchallenge.org/ (opened): the controls bundle that can be downloaded now is `context_A.h5ad`, `context_B.h5ad`, `context_C.h5ad`; “Each context is a different cell line. We don't publish which one.” The final phase “is three different cell lines, released as contexts D, E and F with their own control files.”

**Verified** from the search-index text of https://virtualcellchallenge.org/ (the site is client-rendered; a full HTML save was not made): control profiles now described are 18,400 unperturbed cells for each of **three** contexts; the final test set is dated **22 October 2026**.

**Inferred** from those three statements together: D/E/F non-targeting profiles are part of the 22 October bundle, not a file that is already public. No sentence was found that says a tissue label or an earlier preview will be posted.

## Five to take first for a context encoder

1. **DepMap 26Q1 protein-coding TPM CSV (305 MB).** Broadest cancer-line basal matrix, open, and small enough to use immediately. Risk: bulk poly(A) RNA-seq versus Flex probes, so it will not learn the platform map; the total number of profiles was not on the pages read; CC BY 4.0 is the programme statement, not a line re-read on the 26Q1 file panel.
2. **Tahoe-100M, plate-matched `DMSO_TF` cells only.** About 47 lines left after QC, single-cell, CC0. Risk: Parse barcoding after fixation, median under 2,000 transcripts/cell, 24 h DMSO rather than never-treated cells, and a **338 GB** download. Do not train on the drug arms if the encoder is supposed to see basal state only.
3. **Kinker GSE157220.** 198 lines, 22 cancer types, real 10x Chromium, untreated. Risk: 3′ chemistry and a 3-day co-culture; 56–1,990 cells per line; processed file size was not on GEO’s file list (the list URL 404’d).
4. **MIX-seq DMSO and untreated zips only** (the six named control zips are about 0.37 GB of the 2.26 GB record). Up to 99 lines in one 10x 3′ experiment, CC BY 4.0. Risk: v2 and v3 are mixed across experiments; roughly 100 cells per line; drug-treated siblings sit in the same record and must stay out of a basal encoder.
5. **HipSci non-targeting cells** (the new 499,998-control count above; counts are already the Figshare objects in the 27 September ingestion plan). Thirty-four lines of one cell type, true CRISPRi controls. Risk: 10x 5′ v2, not Flex, and iPSC only, so it teaches donor variation inside one state rather than tissue diversity.

For the Flex-versus-3′ correction, the first matrix to open is **De Simone**, not any of the five above: same-donor PBMC, 10x 3′ v3 and Flex, public. Risk: blood, not a cell line, and the probe-set version is not stated, while human Flex v1.0.1 and v2.0 already differ (18,082 vs 18,132 genes after filtering).
