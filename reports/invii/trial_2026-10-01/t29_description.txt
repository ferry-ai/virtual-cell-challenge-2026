A neural network trained directly on the counts of single cells (negative binomial likelihood on the genes each source measures, a mixture of responding and escaping cells, the context encoded from control cells, the target encoded from biological descriptors of its gene), on 3.35 million cells of public CRISPR screens: Replogle et al. 2022 (K562 and RPE1), Nadig et al. 2025 (Jurkat; HepG2 held out for evaluation), the H1 training and validation data of the 2025 Virtual Cell Challenge, and HipSci iPSC CRISPRi screens. One seed, 3.8 epochs. The effects of the 300 targets of each context are exported from 2,048 control cells of that context and turned into cells with the same generator and settings as trial-22. A technical check of the network path, not tuned on the leaderboard.
