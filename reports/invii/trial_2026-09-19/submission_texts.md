## t07

**Model name:** `trial-07 sc-generator + conditioned linear model x2`

**Description:**

Cells are generated by the same model as trial-03, fitted on each context's own control cells;
no control cell is copied into the prediction. The predicted effect of a target is the output of
a regularised linear model trained on Replogle 2022 pseudobulk effects (K562 genome-wide and
RPE1 essential screens; empirical-Bayes-shrunk log fold changes on the 6,835 genes measured in
K562, RPE1 and HepG2). Its inputs are the target's own K562 effect (32 principal components; 272
of the 300 targets have one), the mean K562 effect of the target's STRING physical partners, the
target's expression in the context's controls and in K562, and a per-gene gate on the K562
effect that is linear in each gene's expression in the context's controls against K562. It was
selected and scored on held-out HepG2 targets (Nadig 2024), a context never used in training,
where it did not beat the trial-03 recipe (difference in predicted score -0.009, 95% interval
-0.036 to +0.017). The effect is applied at amplitude 2.0 with no cis term; the other 11,698
genes of the official axis keep the control level and the target gene is set to a fixed
knockdown. No perturbed data from contexts A, B or C was used.
