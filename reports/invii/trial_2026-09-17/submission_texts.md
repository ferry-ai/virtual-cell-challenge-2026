# Testi delle sottomissioni del 17 settembre

Il nome del modello e la descrizione compaiono in classifica. Devono descrivere ciò che
il file contiene, non ciò che speriamo faccia.

## t02

**Model name:** `trial-02 sc-generator + K562 x1 + cis`

**Description:**

Cells are generated by a model fitted on each context's own control cells: PCA of the
highly variable genes, a new state sampled near an observed one, the composition decoded
as the mean of the 30 nearest control cells, and per-gene Gamma-Poisson dispersion matched
to the controls' zero fractions. No control cell is copied into the prediction. With no
predicted effect this generator makes 0.5 significant genes per perturbation against
held-out controls of the same context (400 cells, Wilcoxon, per-target BH); the generator
of our 2026-09-13 submission made 462.

The predicted effect for a target is its own effect in Replogle 2022 K562 genome-wide
Perturb-seq, read from the single-cell file (1,989,578 cells) as the log ratio of mean
per-cell fractions with an empirical-Bayes shrinkage from the between-cell standard error,
applied at amplitude 1.0; 269 of the 300 targets are covered. On top of it, a CRISPRi cis
term: a gene whose TSS lies within 5 kb of the target's and that K562 measured takes its
own K562 effect, and the remaining neighbours take a median-by-distance prior fitted on
the K562 targets outside the panel. No perturbed data from contexts A, B or C was used.
Amplitudes were chosen by a rule written before the benches existed, on a K562 -> HepG2
transfer bench scored with cell-eval2 0.16.0.

## t03

**Model name:** `trial-03 sc-generator + K562 x2 + cis`

**Description:** the trial-02 model with the transfer amplitude at
2.0 instead of 1.0, which raises the median number of genes the prediction calls significant per perturbation from
39/28/46 to 571/481/560 in contexts A/B/C, and cuts the perturbations calling fewer than ten genes
from 6/9/6 out of 20 to 0/0/1 (measured on the official contexts' own control cells).
The pre-registered rule capped the amplitude at 1.0 because of a K562 bench artefact; this
submission is the same rule without that cap. One second, minor difference: the cis bins were
read from a precomputed distance/effect table rather than refitted on the K562 source targets.
They are the same measurement and agree with the refitted ones to within 0.02 in natural log
(-0.332/-0.231/-0.109/-0.087/-0.036 against -0.328/-0.230/-0.129/-0.080/-0.033).
