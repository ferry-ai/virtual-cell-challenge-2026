A small network that corrects knockdown effects measured in K562 (Replogle et al. 2022, genome-wide) before transferring them to a cell context never seen perturbed. It was trained on public CRISPRi screens of other lines (RPE1, HepG2, Jurkat, KOLF2.1J iPSC, the HipSci iPSC screens, Tian et al. 2019 and 2021, and the 2025 challenge H1 training and validation data) with a contrastive loss: each predicted effect must be closer to its own measured effect than to those of other targets in the same line. The correction is centred over targets and starts from the plain copy. On five line groups held out in turn it improved the copy without losing target discrimination. Cells come from the trial-22 generator at effect scale 2, as trial-31. One seed.
